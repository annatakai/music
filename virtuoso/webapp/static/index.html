Preserving the intention behind the music

Classical music is more than playing the notes written on a page. Every score carries the musical language of a composer—their approach to phrasing, dynamics, tempo, articulation, and expression—shaped by the historical and artistic world in which they composed.

Yet a score cannot capture everything about how music should sound. Much of classical interpretation lives between the notes, passed down through performance traditions, recordings, scholarship, and teaching.

We explore how AI can help preserve and make this interpretive knowledge more accessible. Rather than defining one “correct” performance, we give musicians a reference point for understanding the composer's stylistic world, seeing where their own interpretation differs, and making those artistic choices more intentionally.

Preserve the tradition. Understand the intention. Find your interpretation.
